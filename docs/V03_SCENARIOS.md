# v0.3 scenario classification

**Design only.** The [31-row scenario table](v03_scenarios.csv) annotates the existing nine paper presets, twelve structural presets and ten paired variants at scientific-source baseline `9e65050`. This is a method–target–assumptions map for the planned [v0.3 analysis design](V03_DESIGN.md), not a new numerical campaign, a revised generator, or a frozen comparison grid. Native dimensions, mechanism seeds, target populations, equations and noise remain those of the preset factories. Sample-budget changes do not establish a new identification assumption. The [method inventory](V03_METHODS.md) describes the recipes separately.

The classification answers which observed-data contrast can identify the declared target **under stated assumptions**. `TrainingView` supplies OBS training, trial training/tuning and an optional permitted target-covariate reference. Evaluator access to `tau_target` does not establish identification from those inputs. Nor does mathematical identification establish that a fixed learner, learned representation, linear correction, optimization budget or uncertainty procedure is appropriate.

## Reading the CSV

The CSV is the single row-level classification; this document defines its fields and interpretation. Row order follows [the canonical smoke catalog](../configs/smoke.toml): M0–M11, C01–C10, then the nine paper IDs. No source-baseline Ridge design, sensitivity-factory configuration, empirical dataset or new DGP is added.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `scenario_key` | Exact existing `kind:id`; no condition label or override. |
| `native_d` | Preset dimension after applying the relevant card to its configuration defaults. |
| `target_population` | Declared evaluation covariate law, not the OBS or trial law by default. |
| `target_type` | Population CATE conditional on every displayed covariate; power-likelihood rows explicitly use full `W=(C,Z)`. |
| `target_cate` | Canonical target equation or named neural components; zero-based `x0,x1,...` match the code. |
| `obs_covariates` | OBS covariate law, stated separately so an OBS adjustment route does not assume target coverage silently. |
| `trial_covariates` | Trial covariate law, including observed-X participation selection when active. |
| `support_relation` | `trial_equals_target`, `trial_selected_full_support`, or `target_outside_trial`; this concerns covariates, not effect invariance. |
| `overlap_detail` | Direction is target density divided by trial density. Population support/ratio bounds are distinguished from practical coverage. |
| `trial_propensity` | Known randomized treatment probability, potentially conditional on X. |
| `trial_target_cate` | Whether conditional trial CATE equals target CATE; it does not assert equality of arm means. |
| `obs_hidden_treatment_confounding` | Hidden treatment-selection discrepancy at the preset; hidden variables alone do not imply confounding. |
| `obs_source_shift_0`, `obs_source_shift_1` | OBS potential-outcome mean minus target mean, separated from treatment-selection discrepancy. |
| `obs_target_cate` | Whether the OBS causal contrast equals the target contrast, before OBS treatment-selection bias. |
| `identification_route` | A sufficient observed-data route under its assumptions; not a theorem about every method or every information set. |
| `case_roles` | Semicolon-separated descriptive roles defined below; roles are not a partition of identified/unidentified problems. |
| `estimator_caveat` | Principal distinction between identification and the implemented recipe, or between source-specific assumptions. |
| `target_reference` | Role support and its native default; it is not an instruction to override the preset. |
| `noise_inference` | Native noise and existing heavy-tail policy; ordinary rows make no new v0.3 inference guarantee. |
| `evidence` | Pipe-separated repository-relative `path#symbol` references. Brackets select a card entry; dots select a method. These are source locators, not HTML anchors. |

`expit_clip(z)` denotes the implementation's `expit(clip(z,-30,30))`; `Phi^-1` is the standard-normal quantile. `delta0` and `delta1` denote the omitted CorNet corrections. In C07/C08, `m_part=E[V | trial]` under `V~N(0,1)` with participation probability `expit_clip(2V)`; it is positive. Structural coordinates are axis-aligned Gaussian coordinates at every native preset, with no predictable hidden component (`u_predictability=0`).

## Method assumptions and target contrasts

Let `m_obs,a=E[Y|T=a,X,OBS]` be the observed arm regression, `mu_obs,a=E[Y(a)|X,OBS]` the OBS causal arm mean, and `mu_target,a=E_target[Y(a)|X]` the target causal arm mean. Then `c_a=m_obs,a-mu_obs,a` is treatment-selection bias and `d_a=mu_obs,a-mu_target,a` is OBS source shift, as defined by the [data protocol](PROTOCOL.md#confounding-and-source-outcome-shift). The observational regression contrast equals target CATE plus `(c_1-c_0)+(d_1-d_0)`. A randomized trial estimates its own conditional contrast. Transport to the target additionally requires conditional-effect equality and trial positivity on the target support. These are model assumptions available to an analysis; the simulator's truth only labels whether its construction satisfies them.

| Method or strategy | Sufficient target-identification assumptions | Preset implications and recipe limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RCT-only conditional-effect estimation, including RCT T- and DR-learners | Randomized treatment with positive arm probabilities; conditional trial–target CATE equality; trial support covers the target. Conditional regression also requires suitable estimation regularity. | This target-wide route applies except the two normal-target KPS rows and C08. Known variable propensity in multivariate Integrative R is valid trial randomization conditional on X. A DR pseudo-outcome does not repair absent support, failed effect transport, or a misspecified final CATE regression. |
| OBS-only conditional adjustment | OBS covariate support covers the target, positive observational treatment probabilities, no residual treatment confounding given X, and OBS–target CATE equality. | M0 and C04–C08 have this route at their native settings: OBS and target share `N(0,I_d)` covariates and OBS treatment probabilities are clipped to `[0.01,0.99]`. C04 has an arm-common OBS shift; C07/C08 have zero-mean hidden participation contributions in OBS. CorNet and CVCI have no hidden treatment-selection confounding but fail OBS–target effect equality. |
| Source-blind pooled armwise regressions | A sufficient condition is common conditional arm means across fitting sources, along with valid within-source adjustment and positivity. | Equal CATE alone is insufficient: arm-common source offsets can combine with different source proportions within treatment arms. C04 and C07 illustrate why a CATE-neutral shift does not automatically validate naive pooling. Accidental cancellation is not a general guarantee. |
| OBS representation with trial correction, or another fusion correction | Trial-to-target transport/support plus an identifiable and estimable discrepancy relation, or a separately stated extrapolation restriction. | Arbitrary OBS CATE shift need not defeat trial-based identification. It can defeat a fixed correction class or a representation that discards needed features; C09/C10 target that distinction. CorNet's source-related DGP shares the network representation by construction, but the finite fitted adapter has no automatic correctness guarantee. |
| Target density weighting or target adaptation | The method must receive a declared target-law covariate sample or another specified source of target-distribution information, plus the needed density-ratio/positivity conditions. | Query `eval_X` is prediction-only. An optional structural target reference may be enabled in a later declared design; paper presets have no such configuration role. Density weighting alone cannot identify effects outside trial support or repair C08 effect nontransport. |
| Pinned DataFusionPPI software applicability | The current adapter additionally requires a constant known trial treatment probability. | Multivariate Integrative R is N/A for this adapter because its known randomization probability varies with X. This is an input-contract incompatibility, not a general nonidentification claim. Other runtime or model failures remain separate. |

Recipe evidence: [`methods.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/methods.py), symbols `TLearner.fit_predict`, `RCTDRLearner.fit_predict`, and `DataFusionPPI.fit_predict`; [`cornet.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/cornet.py), symbol `OfficialCorNet.fit_predict`. See the [method inventory](V03_METHODS.md) for exact options and installed-source evidence. None of these assumption statements is a new adapter theorem or permission to change upstream code.

## Roles and consequential distinctions

`point_estimation` denotes a trial-supported target-CATE route conditional on documented effect transport and positivity. `extrapolation_stress` denotes target covariates outside trial support, requiring additional structure for target-wide estimation. `effect_transport_violation` flags a source–target causal-effect discrepancy; the CSV identifies whether that source is OBS or trial. `descriptive_heavy_tail` preserves M11's separate inference treatment. Multiple roles can apply.

**OBS effect shift differs from trial effect nontransport.** M10, the CorNet translation and CVCI have OBS–target effect shifts while trial CATE equals target CATE. C08 instead has `tau_trial-tau_target=2*m_part>0`: hidden participation selects an effect modifier with arm loadings `(-1,+1)`. C07 has the same participation law and loadings `(+1,+1)`, so both trial arm means shift while their difference is preserved. C08 is not globally unidentified: its OBS treatment is unconfounded given X and its OBS causal contrast equals the target contrast. Using that route requires those assumptions; a randomized trial alone does not supply them. Evidence: [`structural_dgp.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/structural_dgp.py), `STRUCTURAL_ADDENDUM_CARDS`, `_draw_participation_hidden`, `_rct_participation_moments`, `_draw_source`, and `structural_truth_at`.

**Normal-target KPS rows require extrapolation.** Both `experimental_grounding_kps` and `integrative_r_learner_kps_normal_target_extension` evaluate a normal target against a Uniform[-1,1] trial. Target-wide nonparametric trial identification therefore fails even though the constructed conditional effects agree. Quadratic effect truth and linear OBS discrepancy can support a separately declared parametric analysis; their availability as evaluator truth is not an assumption granted to every learner. `integrative_r_learner_kps_code` instead has the paper/code Uniform[-1,1] target and the same trial law; its OBS covariates are normal. Evidence: [`paper_dgp.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/paper_dgp.py), `_draw_covariates`, `_kps_truth`, `PAPER_TARGET_POPULATIONS`, and the relevant `PAPER_DGP_CARDS` entries; [paper cards](PAPER_DGPS.md#integrative-r-learner-three-variants) preserve the extension's provenance.

**Full support is not a promise of useful overlap.** M9 selects trial covariates using a two-level positive acceptance function; its target/trial density ratio is bounded above by `exp(2)`, while the effect region is underrepresented. Multivariate Integrative R and Elastic use observed-X selection over Gaussian support. The actual `_sigmoid` clips logits, giving the implemented selection floor `expit(-30)` and a finite bound `E_target[s]/expit(-30)` for target/trial density ratio. That potentially very large bound does not establish well-conditioned overlap or adequate finite-trial coverage. An unclipped logistic selection law has no uniform positive floor over Gaussian covariates. Evidence: [`structural_dgp.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/structural_dgp.py), `_selection_acceptance`, `_draw_covariates`, `_geometry`; [`paper_dgp.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/paper_dgp.py), `_sigmoid`, `_draw_covariates`, `_integrative_r_truth`, and `_elastic_truth`. Structural stored density diagnostics are evaluation-normalized numerical quantities, not exact population density ratios; the support statements here follow the sampling equations.

**Targets and information remain native.** Power-likelihood scoring conditions on full `W=(C,Z)` and includes `(rho1-rho0)*(Z-1-C)` at native noise standard deviation one. Its subgroup effect `0.1+0.1*C` is auxiliary truth, not the scored CATE. The paper-text variant adds equal OBS shifts `d0=d1=0.25`; the Gaussian supplement variant has `d0=d1=0`. Structural `n_target_reference=0` is the native default. `PaperDGPConfig` has no `n_target_reference` field, and `generate_paper_problem` supplies no target reference; a blanket override would change the contract rather than harmonize it. Evidence: [`paper_dgp.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/paper_dgp.py), `_power_truth`, `PaperDGPConfig`, `generate_paper_problem`; [`structural_dgp.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/structural_dgp.py), `StructuralDGPConfig`, `generate_structural_problem`.

**Heavy tails concern inference as well as learning.** M11 retains `Student-t(3)/sqrt(3)` outcome noise, multiplied by `1+0.5*abs(x0)`, and its discontinuous CATE. C03 changes only noise shape to Gaussian at matched conditional variance; it remains a separate preset. The [existing catalog policy](CATALOG_STUDY.md#m11-remains-a-descriptive-heavy-tail-stress-test) withholds M11 mean-MCSE, mean-t intervals and mean-t p-values while retaining descriptive means and empirical quantiles. This annotation carries that limitation forward without freezing a v0.3 testing family or asserting finite score variance for every other recipe. Evidence: [`structural_dgp.py`](../src/obs_rct_benchmark/structural_dgp.py), `MECHANISM_CARDS`, `STRUCTURAL_ADDENDUM_CARDS`, `_draw_noise`; [`analyze_catalog_study.py`](../scripts/analyze_catalog_study.py), `HEAVY_TAIL_PRESET`, `HEAVY_TAIL_REASON`, and `inference_reason`.
