Verification is specific to a producer, environment and check. Package **0.3.0a4** registers 16 recipes; each reported study retains its frozen source and analysis. The [technical report](REPORT.md) combines their scientific interpretation, while the records below define what was checked.

Native-call agreement checks an adapter against an author implementation. Independent target/score arithmetic checks declared equations and reductions. Saved-prediction replay and fresh fitting have different scopes; neither implies empirical validity, unseen-mechanism performance or independent estimator correctness.

## Metrics and independence

Per-seed root-PEHE is the square root of CATE MSE; mean root-PEHE differs from square root of mean MSE. Frozen study analyses govern applicability, heavy tails and multiplicity. Catalog M11 has no mean-MCSE, mean-t interval or p-value. Seed-block Monte Carlo intervals are not uncertainty intervals for individual CATE predictions or evidence of learner uncertainty calibration. See the [metric contract](PROTOCOL.md).

Generic runs resolve [metric-specific permissions](INFERENCE.md) before fitting and record them in the manifest/summary. Student-t(3) noise blocks mean-MSE uncertainty, including mixed summaries. Root-PEHE is separate: its variance can be finite when MSE variance is infinite, while fitted-method moments may remain unestablished. Canonical defaults assume finite score and paired-difference variance for eight generic recipes; other settings need explicit declarations. Arithmetic checks establish enforcement, not those moment conditions or confidence-interval coverage.

Truth follows declared equations, independently of estimator fitting. Component checks use finite-support Bayes sums, rational odds, finite-population conditional means and hand-calculated scores. Sequential checks perturb purchased outcomes and reject hidden-label access, duplicate/excess queries and incomplete/tampered records while preserving costs after failure.

External adapters bind locked checkouts, file hashes and import resolution. Forest additionally binds tracked raw Git blobs, the installed `grf` package, selected dependency runtime files, loaded namespaces/native libraries and Rscript. These observed-byte checks do not prove compiler correspondence or authenticate the whole operating-system/runtime dependency chain. External code remains separately obtained under its own terms. Source-restricted APIs physically omit excluded samples; GP controls also exclude them from hyperparameter fitting.

Fresh seeds measure replication within inspected mechanisms. Replay is not independent empirical validation. No actual IHDP/ACIC file or unseen-mechanism challenge is evaluated here; extreme custom quadrature, mixture, selection or callback settings require their own numerical checks.

## Reproduction

Use each packet's frozen producer/environment and a fresh output directory; source fingerprints prevent resuming retained trees under different code. Optional dependencies and upstream checkouts are described in [native adapter instructions](V03_ADAPTERS.md) and the [README](../README.md). Analysis uses `.[analysis]`. Source checks are `python -m pytest` and `python scripts/check_release.py`; substantial tests and fitting belong in allocated compute jobs. Study commands are in [catalog](CATALOG_STUDY.md), [controlled GP](GP_SOURCE_PILOT.md), [native panel](../results/native_panel/README.md), [matched-M2](REFERENCE_STUDY.md#run-and-reproduce) and [recruitment](../results/adaptive_reference/SCIENTIFIC_RESULT.md#reproduce-and-verify).

CI runs source/core checks, catalog/Gamma/file examples, 24-cell adaptive smoke/replay, notebooks and public catalog/native-panel scalar arithmetic. It does not rerun full campaigns, native-panel raw truth/prediction checks or configured native integrations. Full arrays, environments and logs remain on research storage.
