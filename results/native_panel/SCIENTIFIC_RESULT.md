# OBS access and competitiveness answer different questions

This native/default development comparison has finished with **8,350 successful fits, 64 failures, 31 timeouts and three preserved missing attempts** out of **8,448 requested fits**. The six inspected mechanisms, two RCT budgets, eleven recipes and 64 seeds were fixed before comparative fitting. Separate incomplete-collection verification reconstructs the trial targets and successful prediction scores. The original complete-union contract remains unmet, and **all 72 joint decisions and the simultaneous precision statement are withheld**. Mean-risk uncertainty was not established for this panel.

## Improving a backbone does not establish competitiveness

In M2 at RCT128, GP Full has mean CATE-MSE **0.35128**, below its matched RCT-only GP's **0.53675**. The RCT-only causal forest has mean **0.18386**. At RCT512 the corresponding values are **0.16670**, **0.18775** and **0.084582**. Every one of these method-condition rows has 64 successful seeds. These are descriptive means for the two stated conditions, without a significance or global-ranking claim.

The GP comparison measures the effect of granting OBS access to an admitted common backbone. Its kernel and noise hyperparameters are fitted separately using each procedure's permitted data; this is not a posterior update at fixed fitted hyperparameters. GP-versus-forest and the other fusion-versus-forest comparisons assess complete procedures with different models and native fitting rules. CorNet, PPI and Integrative R have no admitted matched source-excluded controls in this panel. Their differences from forest cannot isolate the contribution of OBS.

## Targets, information and uncertainty

All recipes are scored on trial-population CATE, with independent trial-law queries. **C08 trial target is different from canonical catalog C08**; their scalar errors cannot be substituted. Fusion receives 512 OBS outcomes and 128/512 RCT fitting outcomes; RCT references receive no OBS outcomes. No extra tuning labels are supplied. Equal RCT-label access does not imply equal model capacity, tuning or compute expenditure.

All [132 method-condition rows](method_summaries.csv), [72 prespecified comparison rows](paired_comparisons.csv), [per-seed score/status shards](scalar_records.csv) and [98 non-successful or missing identities](non_successful_fits.csv) remain available. Incomplete paired means and probabilities are withheld. Complete pointwise strict-win summaries are descriptive only; stored pointwise p-values do not authorize joint decisions. Method mean root-PEHE averages seedwise square roots and is distinct from the square root of method mean MSE. No global raw-MSE ranking across different target scales is introduced.
